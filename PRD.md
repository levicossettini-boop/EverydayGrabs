This Product Requirements Document (PRD) outlines the specifications for a high-converting landing page for** ** **Everydaygrabs** , specifically focusing on the** ** **Pop Out Bed Desk** . Drawing from high-conversion e-commerce examples like** ****Life Interiors** (furniture layout),** ****Drink Mockly** (bold storytelling), and** ****Idle Hour Matcha** (minimalist aesthetic), this page is designed to turn Meta Ad traffic into immediate sales.

---

# PRD: Everydaygrabs "Pop Out Bed Desk" Landing Page

## 1. Project Overview

* **Product:** Pop Out Bed Desk (Standard, Pro, and XL variants).
* **Primary Goal:** Maximize conversion rate (CVR) for cold and retargeted Meta Ad traffic.
* **Target Audience:** Remote workers, students, binge-watchers, and individuals seeking ergonomic comfort without leaving bed.

## 2. Visual & Brand Identity

* **Aesthetic:** Clean, minimalist, and "cozy-modern." Use a high-contrast color palette for Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons (e.g., black or deep green against a neutral cream/white background), similar to the high-visibility buttons used by** ** **Idle Hour Matcha** .
* **Imagery:** High-resolution lifestyle photography showing the desk in actual "in-bed" use cases. Mobile-responsive image scaling is mandatory to ensure fast load times and visual clarity on smartphones.

## 3. Page Structure & User Experience (UX)

### A. Global Header (Sticky)

* **Logo:** Left-aligned Everydaygrabs logo.
* **CTA:** A "Shop Now" button that anchors to the product variant section.
* **Cart Icon:** With a real-time item counter, similar to the functionality seen in** ** **Life Interiors** .

### B. Hero Section (The "Hook")

* **Headline:** "The desk for when you don't feel like sitting at a desk."
* **Sub-headline:** Ergonomic, foldable, and built for your ultimate comfort. Work, eat, or watch from the comfort of your bed.
* **Visual:** A 10-second looping high-quality video (auto-play, muted) or a hero image showing the "Pop Out" action.
* **Primary CTA:** "Get Yours – Starting at $[Price]" (High-contrast button).

### C. Social Proof Bar (Trust Signals)

* **Elements:** "As Seen In" logos or star ratings (e.g., "4.9/5 stars from 2,000+ happy loungers").
* **Trust Badges:** "Free Shipping," "30-Day Money Back Guarantee," and "Secure Checkout."

### D. Problem vs. Solution Section

* **Problem:** Images showing neck strain, laptops overheating on blankets, and spilled drinks.
* **Solution:** The Pop Out Bed Desk solving these issues with its stable surface, heat-venting design, and dedicated cup holder.

### E. Product Showcase & Variant Selector

This section must mirror the granular detail found in** ****Life Interiors'** product listings.

* **Variant Options:**
  * **Standard:** The classic lightweight model.
  * **Pro:** Features adjustable height and tilt angles.
  * **XL:** Extra wide for dual-monitor or large gaming laptops.
* **Interactive Elements:**
  * **Dynamic Image Swapping:** Clicking a color or size variant must update the main image immediately.
  * **Clear Pricing:** Boldly display "Compare at" pricing to show savings (e.g., ~~$79~~ $49).
* **Direct "Add to Cart":** Implement a "Buy It Now" interception to skip the cart and go straight to checkout for higher conversion.

### F. Deep Dive: Features & Benefits

* **Heat Dissipation:** Built-in cooling vents.
* **Stability:** Non-slip "W" legs for mattress stability.
* **Storage:** Side drawer for pens, chargers, or snacks.

### G. Customer Testimonials (Social Proof)

* **Format:** "Verified Buyer" reviews with photos. Include a "Product Review Count" component similar to the one used by** ****Drink Mockly** to build rapid authority.

### H. FAQ Section (Friction Reduction)

* **Questions:**
  * "Does it fit over a King-size bed?"
  * "How much weight can it hold?"
  * "Is any assembly required?" (Answer: No, it pops out of the box).

### I. Footer

* **Newsletter Signup:** Offer a "10% off your first order" incentive to capture leads who don't convert immediately, similar to** ****Idle Hour Matcha's** lead-gen strategy.
* **Links:** Shipping Policy, Refund Policy, and "Track My Order".

---

## 4. Technical Requirements

* **Mobile-First Design:** 80%+ of Meta traffic is mobile; the page must be optimized for thumb-scrolling and fast mobile loading.
* **Tracking:** Full integration of Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, and conversion tracking (e.g., Elevar) to measure ROAS.
* **Performance:** All images must use lazy loading and modern formats (WebP) to maintain a PageSpeed score of 90+.
* **Accessibility:** Ensure high color contrast and ARIA labels for screen readers.

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)

* **Target Conversion Rate:** 3.5% – 5.0%.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** Goal of $65+ (driven by Pro/XL upsells).
* **Bounce Rate:** Maintain below 40%.
